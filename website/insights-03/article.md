# DAMAC Hills, Dubai: What Daily Life Is Built Around

[hero: community-pool.jpg, alt "Aerial view of a lagoon-style pool with sun loungers in DAMAC Hills, Dubai"]

When people start looking at Dubai, they often begin with the apartment: the view, the size, the finish. After a few viewings most of them realise the community matters just as much. It decides how you spend your mornings, where the children play, and whether you walk anywhere at all.

DAMAC Hills is a good example of a community designed around a lifestyle rather than a single building. Here is what daily life there is organised around, and what to check before you put a home on your shortlist.

## 1. The golf course

[image: golf.jpg, alt "Golf fairway at sunset with residential buildings on the horizon"]

A championship golf course runs through the middle of the community. For many residents that means a view of fairways instead of a road, and a quieter, greener feel than the city centre. You don't have to play golf to benefit from it, but it is worth asking which side of a building faces the course and which faces the road.

## 2. Water as a centrepiece

[image: wave-pool.jpg, alt "A beach-style pool lined with palm trees and umbrellas"]

Lagoon-style pools and a wave pool give the community a resort rhythm on ordinary weekdays. Families tend to use them most in the cooler months and in the evenings.

**Ask:** which pools are open to all residents, and which belong to a particular building or cluster?

## 3. Space to walk

[image: lawns.jpg, alt "Wide lawns and a walking path between residential buildings"]

Wide lawns and walking paths run between the residential buildings. In a city where many people drive everywhere, being able to walk to a park is a real quality-of-life difference.

## 4. Stables, skate park and sports

[image: stables.jpg, alt "Riding paddocks with white fencing inside the community"]

DAMAC Hills includes stables and riding paddocks, a skate park and a range of sports courts. For families with active children this can matter more than an extra bedroom.

## 5. Evenings outside

Dubai life shifts outdoors once the heat drops. Communities with good outdoor spaces feel very different after 6 pm from those built only around a mall.

## Questions to ask before you shortlist

1. How far is the home from the amenities you will actually use, on foot?
2. Which facilities are community-wide and which are building-only?
3. What are the service charges, and what do they cover? Ask for the latest figures in writing.
4. Is the home ready, or off-plan? If off-plan, read the official contract and payment schedule yourself.
5. Visit at two different times of day if you can, or ask for a live video walk-through.
